Bounded state

Up to 128 chunks of 512 bytes. ACKs must match session, total, active sequence and expected reply kind. Only one frame is outstanding; retries resend that frame.

Exact reassembly

The receiver accepts in-order chunks, checks duplicate content and replies to duplicates without appending twice. FIN carries the SHA-256 digest of the full payload.

Checksums and deterministic fixture sessions provide no authentication or protection against an adversarial peer.

03 / FINAL HANDSHAKE

Receiver completion and sender confirmation

Lost first DONE

The receiver retains completed session state and responds to repeated FIN. A later DONE lets the sender confirm completion.

Every DONE lost

The receiver can finish while the sender fails its retry budget. The trace reports both outcomes. There is no claim of exactly-once external effects.

05 / MEASUREMENT AND REPRODUCTION

Local socket evidence and limits

Measured execution

Three ephemeral loopback UDP sockets: sender, receiver and relay. Faults drop, duplicate, corrupt or delay matching datagrams. Delayed ACKs may arrive after later replies.

Evidence boundaries

JSONL contains application instrumentation, not PCAP or NIC timestamps. Send records occur after sendto; cross-thread log order is not a causality clock. Fixed-order finite trials on one host do not estimate WAN performance.

The receiver stays available through the sender retry window and a 110 ms observation tail. Restart recovery and durable sessions are outside this implementation.
